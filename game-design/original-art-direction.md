# Original Art Direction: Neon Divide

## Goal

Build an original crime-economy sandbox with grounded urban realism, premium 4K materials, and high-poly hero assets. The game should feel dangerous, aspirational, and intimate without copying the identity, branding, characters, map, props, or visual signatures of any existing title.

## Core Fantasy

The player rises from a forgotten industrial district into a self-made underground logistics empire. The city is split between old brick industry, washed-out coastal sprawl, luxury hill enclaves, and freight infrastructure. The tone is street-level and tactile rather than satirical.

## Visual Pillars

1. Weathered luxury
Luxury objects exist in spaces that are stained, improvised, and under pressure.

2. Human-scale urban density
Alleys, storefronts, garages, rooftops, and interiors should feel packed and lived in.

3. Analog grime with modern polish
Cracked tiles, oxidized metals, sun-faded plastics, and grease buildup paired with physically plausible 4K materials.

4. Regional originality
Avoid direct west-coast parody. Use a fictional port city with mixed architectural influences: maritime warehouses, deco civic blocks, elevated roadways, compact duplex neighborhoods, and blufftop mansions.

## Color Script

- Industrial district: oil black, faded teal, rust orange, sodium-vapor amber
- Retail streets: dirty cream, sunburnt red, cool gray signage, fluorescent green accents
- Port logistics: weathered navy, container red, desaturated yellow, wet concrete
- Luxury zone: limestone, smoked glass, bronze trim, muted olive landscaping
- Night atmosphere: cyan reflections, orange practical lights, selective magenta neon

## Material Standard

- Hero props: 4K albedo, normal, roughness, metallic, AO
- Reusable mid-tier props: 2K or 4K depending on screen proximity
- Trim sheets for storefront frames, industrial beams, interior wall systems, and roadway barriers
- Decal library for leaks, posters, tire marks, paint wear, gum, grime streaks, and patched concrete

## Modeling Standard

- High-poly source for all hero assets
- Clean low-poly game mesh with baked normals
- Bevel all visible edges
- Distinct wear logic by asset category
- Modular architecture snapped to grid for repeatable assembly

## Original Districts

### 1. Breakwater Yard

Player starting district. Repair garages, scrap fencing, pawn windows, side-street apartments, truck lots, and flood channels.

### 2. Cinder Market

Dense retail blocks with laundromats, discount marts, smoke shops, cramped back rooms, rooftop signage, and alley service entries.

### 3. Marrow Port

Container stacks, bonded warehouses, forklift bays, cold storage, rail spurs, mooring edges, and customs fencing.

### 4. Saint Vela Heights

Hill roads, retaining walls, luxury gates, modern villas, hidden garages, private security booths, and glass-heavy interiors.

## Asset Personality

- Vehicles should feel sourced from believable local economies: compact delivery vans, dented sedans, contractor pickups, aging coupes, premium SUVs
- Interiors should be cluttered with purpose, not random noise
- Tools and industrial equipment should show maintenance history
- Packaging and labels must be original and fictional
- Signage should use a custom place-name system tied to the districts above

## Do Not Copy

- Existing game logos, UI language, product naming, map layout, factions, character silhouettes, or signature props
- Distinctive architecture lifted from another game’s screenshots
- Promotional key art composition that closely tracks another title

## Audio and Motion Cues For Asset Authors

- Loose hanging cables, rattling shutters, vibrating vents, rolling dock chains
- Fans, strip lights, security cameras, and cheap signage should have subtle animation hooks
- Doors, gates, and garage shutters should be modeled for real traversal states
